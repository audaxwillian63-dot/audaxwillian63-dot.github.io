X-OM — short stays in Dar es Salaam

Also known as Makazi · Operated by DAXLLIAM ELECTRONIC STORE, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

X-OM is a mobile marketplace that connects guests looking for a short stay in Dar es Salaam with hosts who have a room, apartment or house to rent for a few nights or weeks.

For guests

  • Browse stays on a map and see photos, prices and reviews.
  • Book and pay in the app by card or mobile money (processed by PayMe).
  • Message or call your host inside the app.
  • Get directions to your stay and, if you choose, share your live location during the trip for safety.

For hosts

  • List your property with photos, prices and availability.
  • Receive booking requests and chat with guests.
  • Verify your identity once to receive payouts.
  • See all fees clearly in the app before you publish.

Signing in

You can create an account with your email address and a password, or sign in with Google. If you use Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile photo to create and secure your account. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
