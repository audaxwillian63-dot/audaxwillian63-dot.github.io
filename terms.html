1. What X-OM is

X-OM is an online marketplace that connects people looking for a short stay in Dar es Salaam ("guests") with people who offer accommodation ("hosts"). Hosts create listings and provide the accommodation. X-OM does not own, manage or inspect listed properties unless we clearly say so. The booking for a stay is between the guest and the host; X-OM provides the platform, handles payments through our payment provider, and offers support.
