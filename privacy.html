1. Who we are

X-OM is a short-stay rental marketplace for Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. It is operated by DAXLLIAM ELECTRONIC STORE, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania ("X-OM", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy, as the data controller under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and its regulations.

This policy covers the X-OM mobile and web app and this website. X-OM connects guests with hosts; hosts, not X-OM, provide the accommodation.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, profile photo and whether you use X-OM as a guest, a host or both. If you sign up with email, you also create a password. We do not store it in readable form; our authentication provider keeps only a secure hash.
  • Google sign-in details: if you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account identifier. See section 7.
  • Host listings: property descriptions, photos, amenities, prices, availability, house rules and the property's location.
  • Host identity verification (KYC): before a host can receive payouts, we ask for identity details such as a NIDA or voter ID number, photos of an identity document and a selfie, plus payout details (for example a mobile money number or bank account). We use these only to confirm identity, prevent fraud, pay hosts and meet legal duties.
  • Bookings: dates, number of guests, prices, fees, discounts, booking status and cancellation details.
  • Messages and calls: messages you send in the app, including photos and voice notes. For voice and video calls we keep call records such as who called whom, when, and for how long.
  • Reviews and ratings you write, and reviews written about you.
  • Support and complaints: what you tell us when you contact us or open a complaint.

Information created when you use X-OM

  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment provider, PayMe, by card or mobile money. We receive payment references, amounts and status (for example paid, failed or refunded). We do not receive or store your full card number or card security code.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device location to show nearby stays and give directions. Live location sharing during a trip is optional and explained in section 6.
  • Device and technical data: device type, operating system, app version, language, IP address, a notification token so we can send you push notifications, and error logs that help us fix problems.
  • Voice search: if you use voice search, your phone's own speech service (for example from Google or Apple) turns your speech into text. We receive the text of your search.

The app stores a small amount of data on your device to keep you signed in and remember settings such as language. We do not use third-party advertising trackers in the app or on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show listings, handle bookings, and process payments, fees, refunds and host payouts.
  • To verify hosts' identity before payouts and to prevent fraud, scams and misuse.
  • To let guests and hosts message and call each other about a booking.
  • To give directions to a stay and, only if you agree, support live location sharing for safety.
  • To send you notifications about bookings, messages, payments and your account. You can turn push notifications off in your phone settings.
  • To publish reviews and keep them honest.
  • To handle support requests, complaints and disputes. If a user, message or listing is reported, we may review the relevant messages and records to investigate.
  • To keep financial and booking records and meet our legal, tax and regulatory duties.
  • To understand and improve how X-OM works.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Our legal grounds

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 we process personal data only where we have a lawful reason, mainly:

  • To perform our contract with you (for example running your account, bookings and payments);
  • Your consent (for example live location sharing and push notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that already happened;
  • Legal obligations (for example keeping financial records or answering lawful requests from authorities);
  • Legitimate interests that do not override your rights (for example preventing fraud and keeping the platform safe).

Identity documents and payout details are sensitive, so we collect them only from hosts who want payouts, limit who can see them, and use them only for verification, payouts, fraud prevention and legal duties.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other users, when needed for a booking: guests and hosts see each other's name, profile photo and booking details, and can message and call each other. Listings and reviews are visible to other users. A host's identity documents and payout details are never shown to guests.
  • Service providers who run parts of X-OM for us:
    • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage and server functions;
    • PayMe: card and mobile money payments and payouts;
    • Agora: carrying voice and video calls in real time;
    • Google: Google sign-in and push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging);
    • Map tile providers: loading map images, which means they receive your IP address and the area of the map you view.
    These providers may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities and legal requests: when the law requires it, or where needed to protect people's safety, prevent fraud, or defend legal claims.
  • Business changes: if our business is sold or reorganised, your data may pass to the new owner, who must continue to protect it.

6. Location and live location sharing

X-OM uses your device location only if you allow it in your phone settings. You can use the app without location access, but maps and directions will be less useful.

Live location sharing is optional. When you start navigation to a stay you have booked, the app asks for your permission before sharing your live location. If you agree, your position is shared while the trip is active so that X-OM can help keep you safe. Sharing stops automatically when you arrive, or earlier if you end the trip. We do not track your location in the background outside an active trip. We keep trip records for a limited time for safety and support.

7. Google user data

If you sign in with Google, X-OM asks Google only for basic sign-in information (the openid, email and profile permissions). This gives us:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your profile photo, and
  • a Google account identifier.

How we use it: only to create your X-OM account, sign you in, fill in your profile (name and photo, which you can change), and contact you about your account and bookings.

How we store it: in our Supabase database and authentication system, protected by access controls.

How we share it: your name and profile photo are shown to hosts or guests you book with, as part of your profile. We do not share Google user data with anyone else except the service providers listed in section 5 who help us run X-OM, or where the law requires it.

What we do not do: we do not get your Google password, and we do not access your contacts, Gmail, Google Drive, calendar or any other Google data. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers. Our staff do not read it except with your permission, for security, or to comply with the law.

Limited Use: X-OM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove X-OM's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings (Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services). This does not delete your X-OM account; see section 12 for that.

8. Where your data is stored

Some of our service providers (such as Supabase, Agora and Google) store or process data on servers outside Tanzania. Where we transfer personal data outside Tanzania, we do so as permitted by the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 and rely on providers that apply recognised security and data protection safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your personal data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data, except for records we must keep for a longer period, for example booking and payment records required by tax and financial law, and information needed to handle open disputes, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations. We keep those records only as long as needed and then delete or anonymise them.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls that limit who can see data, and restricted access to identity documents. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

11. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data, subject to the records we must keep;
  • object to or ask us to limit certain processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example to location sharing or notifications);
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania if you believe we have not handled your data properly.

To use these rights, email audaxwillian63@gmail.com or use Help in the app. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within the time the law requires.

12. Deleting your account

You can ask to delete your X-OM account at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings > Delete my account; or
  • By email: write to audaxwillian63@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account".

Once your request is processed, your profile is removed and you can no longer sign in. We delete or anonymise your personal data, except the limited records described in section 9. If you have upcoming bookings or unpaid amounts, we may need to settle those first.

13. Children

X-OM is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as X-OM changes or the law changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
